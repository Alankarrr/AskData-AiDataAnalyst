# Shipping and Delivery SLA (SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT)

## Seller obligations
Sellers must hand each order to the carrier within 3 business days of payment approval. Sellers who miss this handover deadline in more than 8% of orders in a quarter receive a formal warning.

## Estimated delivery date
The estimated delivery date shown to customers is computed from carrier lead time plus a safety buffer of 3 days. A delivery is "late" when it arrives after this date.

## Targets
The company targets a late-delivery rate below 8% of delivered orders in any month.
