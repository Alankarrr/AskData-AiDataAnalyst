# Incident Report: Delivery Delays, November 2017 (SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT)

## Summary
In November 2017 the late-delivery rate rose well above the 8% target. Order volume surged during the Black Friday campaign, and regional carrier capacity did not keep pace.

## Root causes
1. Order volume in the week of Black Friday was far above the normal weekly level.
2. Two regional carriers hit warehouse capacity limits and held parcels for several days.
3. Several sellers shipped late because of stock-picking backlogs.

## Actions taken
- Added a third carrier for high-volume regions from December 2017.
- Extended the estimated-delivery buffer by 2 days during campaign weeks.
- Introduced a seller capacity check before large campaigns.

## Outlook
Delays eased in December 2017 and January 2018 after the extra carrier capacity was in place.
