# KPI Definitions (SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT)

- **Revenue**: sum of item prices of delivered orders. Freight value is not included.
- **Delivered order**: an order whose status is delivered.
- **Active seller**: a seller with at least one delivered order in the last 90 days.
- **Late-delivery rate**: late delivered orders divided by all delivered orders in the period.
- **Customer satisfaction (CSAT)**: average review score on a 1 to 5 scale.
