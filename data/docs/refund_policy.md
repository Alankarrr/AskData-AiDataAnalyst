# Returns and Refund Policy (SYNTHETIC DOCUMENT)

## Refund window
Customers can request a refund within 7 days of receiving their order. Requests made after 7 days are reviewed case by case by customer support.

## Late deliveries
If an order arrives more than 10 days after the estimated delivery date, the customer is entitled to a full refund of the shipping fee. Orders that never arrive within 45 days of purchase are refunded in full.

## Damaged items
Damaged or defective items must be reported with a photo within 7 days. The company will send a replacement or issue a refund at the customer's choice.

## Processing time
Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5 to 10 business days.
